# Developer demonstration

[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/8uiZaVtBBH4) · [Project page](https://shiruifu.online/projects/carrot-duck/)

Duck recalls that petting a cat helped the user feel better, then reveals its fox ears and offers a playful response. The video shows how a remembered experience can take shape through dialogue and character expression. It also shows the conversational interface and management dashboard.

## From the video to the current prototype

To explore a new exchange, follow the [autonomous walkthrough](AUTONOMOUS_WALKTHROUGH.md). Save an invented experience, ask a related question, and compare the generated reply with its cited memory and selected gesture. Then try a refusal and examine how the response changes.

The [evaluation notes](EVALUATION.md) develop the research question: when does recalling a detail feel personally meaningful, and how does its expression affect that interpretation? They describe proposed comparisons and the evidence needed to evaluate them.
